A question looks wrong

Report it from the question in the app and say what looks off. Every report is read and checked against the FAA source.

Subscriptions

  • New phone: open the Full route screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Hangar Hobbs.
  • Refund: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Crewmates

  • Your code is on the Hangar. Share it, or tap Add a crewmate and type someone else's.
  • To remove, block or report someone, open their card on the Hangar.

Deleting your data

Start over in the app clears this phone and keeps your account. To delete the account itself, email us with your crewmate code. See the privacy policy.
